# Audio

This walkthrough takes you from an empty Audio Library to your first saved transcript. You will make a short microphone recording, then find and reuse its text.

## Before you record

Open **Audio** in Nativ’s sidebar. If it is not there, enable the included Audio extension in **Extensions**.

In Audio’s **Model** tab, check that an **Active model** appears. If no speech-to-text model is installed, select **Find models**. [Start the server](/getting-started/starting-server) before you record so Nativ can transcribe when you finish.

## 1. Check your microphone

Open **Record**. Choose your microphone under **Audio source**, then speak briefly: the **Input level** meter should move. For this first recording, turn **Include system audio** off so Nativ records only your microphone.

## 2. Record and complete

Select **Start recording** and say a short note—for example, a decision you need to remember and its next step. macOS may ask for Microphone access. When you are finished, select **Complete**. Nativ saves the audio and creates a transcript.

## 3. Find the result

Open **Library** and select the new recording. Use **Play** to check the audio, open **Transcript** to read it, then **Copy** the text into your notes. Give the recording a descriptive title so you can find it later.

To get summarized notes too, leave **Auto-summary** on before recording and have a language model installed. If a recording has audio but no transcript, select **Transcribe** in the Library to try again.
