# Dictate into any app

Use dictation when you want your words inserted where you are already typing, without making a saved recording in the Audio Library.

1. In Nativ’s **Audio → Model** tab, check that a speech-to-text model is active and the server is running.
2. Open a text field in the app where you want the words to appear. Place the cursor where the sentence should begin.
3. Hold **Fn + Control**, speak, then release the keys. Nativ transcribes your speech, inserts it at the cursor, and copies it to the clipboard.

If you prefer not to hold the keys, open **Audio → Shortcuts** and enable **Hands-free**. Tap the dictation shortcut once to start and again to transcribe. You can change the shortcut on that same screen.

Use **Fn + R** to retry the most recent audio. Your text also appears under **Audio → Library → Recent dictations**. Dictation’s raw audio is deleted after five minutes; its transcript remains until you delete it.

If the shortcut does nothing outside Nativ, allow Nativ **Accessibility** access in macOS System Settings, then check the shortcut currently shown in **Audio → Shortcuts**.
